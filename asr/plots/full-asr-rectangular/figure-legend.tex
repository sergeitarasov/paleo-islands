\noindent\textbf{Dung beetle biogeography.}
Time-calibrated phylogeny of the 271 sampled ingroup specimens.
Internal pie charts show marginal ancestral-range probabilities averaged
across the fitted gr6 and gr7 models using their AIC weights; tip circles
show observed geographic ranges. All 16 geographic states are retained.
A, Africa; E, Eurasia--Oriental; M, Madagascar; U, Australia;
S, paleoislands; R, Mauritius. Compound codes denote ranges spanning
two regions. The time axis gives ages in millions of years (Ma).
Specimen identifiers accompany all tip names.
